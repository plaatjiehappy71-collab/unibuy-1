About us

Connecting businesses, empowering entrepreneurs and making shopping easier.

UniBuy is a South African digital marketplace created to connect buyers, individual sellers, entrepreneurs and small businesses through one accessible online platform.

Our mission is to make it easier for people to discover products, showcase what they sell, reach new customers and grow their businesses through digital opportunities.

We believe that every business deserves an opportunity to be seen, regardless of its size, location or resources. Whether you are an established business, a growing entrepreneur, a student looking to make extra income or an individual selling products, UniBuy provides a platform where your products can reach potential customers.

Through technology, product discovery and digital marketing, we are working towards creating an accessible marketplace that brings sellers and buyers closer together.

Our story

UniBuy was born from an idea of creating opportunities through technology.

The idea was built around a simple observation: many people have products to sell, but finding customers and getting their businesses noticed is not always easy. At the same time, potential buyers often struggle to discover products and sellers in one convenient place. We saw an opportunity to bring these two sides together.

Initially, UniBuy was developed with a strong focus on the university and student community. The vision was to create a digital marketplace where students could discover products, buy from other students and give young entrepreneurs an opportunity to showcase what they offer.

As the project developed, the vision became bigger. We realised that the opportunity extended beyond university campuses. Small businesses, independent sellers and emerging entrepreneurs also needed affordable ways to showcase their products and reach customers. This led to the expansion of UniBuy's vision into a broader marketplace that welcomes businesses and individual sellers.

Today, UniBuy continues to develop with a bigger ambition: to become a platform where businesses grow, products gain visibility and opportunities are created through digital innovation.

What we do

UniBuy provides a digital environment where sellers can showcase their products and buyers can discover what is available. Our platform focuses on:

  • Online marketplace: a platform where individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses can create accounts and showcase the products they sell.
  • Product discovery: a browsing experience that allows users to explore different products, categories and sellers.
  • Business exposure: an opportunity for small businesses and emerging entrepreneurs to introduce their products to a wider audience without having to establish their own online marketplace.
  • Digital marketing and promotion: a marketing approach that uses social media and digital platforms to promote products listed on UniBuy, helping sellers gain additional exposure and reach potential customers.
  • Opportunities for entrepreneurs: entrepreneurship should not be limited by the size of a business. UniBuy creates an opportunity for individuals to start showcasing what they sell and develop their presence online.

Our vision

Our vision is to build a recognised digital marketplace that connects businesses and consumers across South Africa and, eventually, beyond its borders.

We want UniBuy to become more than a place where people list products. We want it to become an ecosystem that supports entrepreneurship, encourages innovation, creates visibility for businesses and makes product discovery easier.

Our mission

Our mission is to use technology and digital marketing to create opportunities for businesses, entrepreneurs and individuals.

We are committed to developing accessible digital solutions that help sellers showcase their products, connect with potential customers and participate in the growing digital economy.

Our values

  • Innovation: continuously improving how people discover and showcase products.
  • Opportunity: creating space for businesses and individuals to grow.
  • Accessibility: making digital marketplace opportunities accessible to more people.
  • Entrepreneurship: supporting people who are building something of their own.
  • Growth: learning, improving and developing alongside our users.
  • Community: building connections between sellers, buyers and businesses.

The founder

Happy Plaatjie, founder and creator of UniBuy.

Happy Plaatjie is a 23-year-old South African entrepreneur, born and raised in Jamestown, Eastern Cape. His educational journey began at Phambili Mzontsundu, where he completed an important part of his early education. He later continued his academic journey at the University of Fort Hare.

Growing up in Jamestown and experiencing different stages of education helped shape his perspective on opportunities, personal development and the importance of creating something meaningful. His interest in entrepreneurship and technology developed around the desire to build something of his own rather than simply wait for opportunities to come.

UniBuy represents that ambition. Instead of keeping an idea on paper, Happy took the initiative to turn it into a working digital platform. The journey involved learning, experimenting, developing the website, addressing technical challenges, improving the user experience and finding ways to introduce the platform to its intended audience.

Building UniBuy has been a journey of learning through experience. From developing its initial concept as a student-focused marketplace to expanding its vision towards supporting small businesses and independent sellers, the project continues to evolve.

For Happy, UniBuy is not simply about selling products online. It is about building opportunities, developing innovative solutions and demonstrating what can be achieved when an idea is supported by determination, creativity and consistent effort. His long-term ambition is to build technology-driven ventures that create value, influence industries and contribute to the future of digital innovation.

The journey ahead

UniBuy is still growing, learning and improving.

We understand that building a meaningful platform takes time, consistency and the willingness to adapt. Every improvement, every seller who joins and every person who discovers a product through the platform contributes to the journey.

Our ambition goes beyond creating another online marketplace. We want to build a platform that gives businesses visibility, encourages entrepreneurship and creates opportunities for people who want to grow.

We are only getting started.
